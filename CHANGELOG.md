# Changelog

## v1.1.0 (2026-11-01)

Much more of the app is in English now. About 1,800 more labels, messages and dialogs that vivo
only ever wrote in Chinese are translated, using vivo's own English names where they exist:

- **Settings**: all settings pages, account & sync, cloud storage, notifications, feedback,
  calendar and recorder settings, and the macOS permission cards.
- **Albums**: album names, import, sorting, delete and replace dialogs, Recently deleted,
  Cloud Albums sync messages and Photo & Video Backup.
- **Recorder**: recordings list, player, transcription, organized text, outline, AI Insights and
  AI summary (including the summary templates).
- **Origin Notes**: editor, version history, AI Creation menu, Documents, share-link pages and the
  BlueLM Copilot window.
- **Calendar**: repeat rules, reminders, birthdays and anniversaries, to-dos and subtasks, and the
  date picker.
- **Device connection, transfer and backup**: connection guides, Infinite collaboration,
  EasyShare notifications, Backup & restore, cross-network dialogs, tray and macOS menus.
- **Main window, search and File Manager**: global and AI search, file search tabs, welcome and
  privacy dialogs, cloud service pages.
- **Common dialogs and toasts**: OK, Cancel, Retry, delete confirmations with correct
  singular/plural ("Delete 3 documents?").

Dates and counts read naturally in English ("2026/5/2 Sat", "Every 2 weeks (Mon, Wed)",
"1 day before, 09:00").

Some less-used screens may still show a little Chinese. Names that come from your phone or
vivo's servers (album names, speaker names, server categories) stay as they are.
